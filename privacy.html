Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

Safe Carry 305 provides beginner firearms training in South Florida. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. By submitting a form on this website, or through one of our Facebook or Instagram lead forms, you agree to this policy.

Information we collect

  • Your first and last name
  • Your email address
  • Your phone number
  • Your class interest (group class or private 1-on-1 class)
  • Booking and payment details when you reserve a class. Payments are processed by Stripe. We do not see or store your full card number.
  • Standard technical logs (such as IP address and browser type) collected by our website host for security and site operation.

How we use your information

  • To send you the class information and pricing you requested
  • To schedule, confirm, and manage your class booking
  • To send class news and updates by email, which you can opt out of at any time
  • To respond to questions you send us

How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with the service providers we use to run the business, such as our email and payment providers, and only as needed to provide our services or comply with the law.

Your choices

  • Every marketing email includes an unsubscribe link.
  • You may ask us to correct or delete your information at any time by emailing support@safecarry305.com.

Children

Our services are intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will post the updated version on this page with a new date.
